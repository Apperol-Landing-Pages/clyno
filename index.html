Frequently Asked Questions

How does the app protect my phone?

The app protects your phone by constantly monitoring it for potential threats, blocking malicious websites and apps, and warning you about suspicious activity. It helps keep your personal data safe, improves overall security, and ensures your device runs smoothly without unnecessary risks.

Is the app safe to use?

Yes, the app is safe to use. It follows strict security and privacy standards, does not access personal data without permission, and works in the background to protect your device without affecting performance.

Do I need a subscription to use the app?

It depends on the features you want. The app can be used for free with basic protection, but a subscription may be required to unlock advanced features and premium support.

How can I cancel my subscription?

To cancel your subscription, go to your account settings in the app, find the “Subscriptions” or “Billing” section, and select “Cancel Subscription.” Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the cancellation. You may also be able to cancel through the app store (Apple App Store) that you used to subscribe. If you need help, contact customer support.

How do I request a refund?

To request a refund, go to the Support or Help section in the app or on the website, then submit a refund request with your order or subscription details and a brief reason for the request. If you purchased through the Apple App Store, you may need to request the refund directly through your app store account purchase history. Follow the instructions provided there to submit your request. If you need help, contact customer support for assistance.
